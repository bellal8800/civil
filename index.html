Calculation basis

Self-weight: b × h × 24 kN/m³.

Factored load: wu = max(1.4D, 1.2D + 1.6L).

Simply supported beam: Mu = wuL²/8 and Vu = wuL/2.

Effective depth: d ≈ h − cover − stirrup diameter − main bar diameter/2.

This Version 1 is an analysis-action module, not a complete structural design approval. Final reinforcement, shear design, development length and detailing will be added in later modules and must be code-checked by a qualified engineer.
